# Chapter 2: Social Media Use and Adolescent Sleep

## 2.1 Introduction

In today's digital age, social media plays a pivotal role in the lives of adolescents. Recent estimates suggest that 95% of teenagers in the United States have access to a smartphone, and the average adolescent spends approximately 2.4 hours per day on social media platforms (Twenge et al., 2018). This chapter delves into the multifaceted relationship between social media use and sleep, aiming to provide a comprehensive understanding of how digital engagement shapes adolescent well-being.

Furthermore, the existing literature presents a nuanced and often conflicting picture. While some studies have reported robust associations between screen time and poor mental health outcomes, others have found that these effects are remarkably small. Notably, a large-scale analysis by Orben & Przybylski (2019) found that the association between digital technology use and adolescent well-being was negligible, explaining at most 0.4% of the variation in well-being. Moreover, a systematic review of 67 studies concluded that the evidence linking screen use to sleep outcomes was consistent but of variable quality (Hale & Guan, 2015).

It is important to note that sleep may serve as a crucial mechanism linking social media use to broader outcomes. Adolescents who use their devices late at night may experience delayed sleep onset, reduced sleep duration, and lower sleep quality, which in turn can affect mood, attention, and academic performance [12]. However, much of this research relies on self-reported screen time, which has been shown to correlate only weakly with objective measures (r = 0.12).

To address these gaps, the present study examines data from 1,200 participants aged 13 to 17, combining objective screen-time logs with actigraphy-based sleep measures. By leveraging these complementary data sources, this chapter seeks to shed light on the temporal dynamics of social media use and sleep, ultimately contributing to a more holistic understanding of adolescent digital life.
